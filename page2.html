Places To Visit In Aruba

Aruba’s beaches are hypnotising, to say the least. Sugary and powdery with crystalline, jade waters on the northwest and more suited to adrenaline lovers on the windward east, the Aruba coast has a spot for every taste and desire. In the first case, the 11-kilometre-long uninterrupted shores from Eagle Beach and Druif Beach to Malmok and Palm Beach are literally dreamy. For a few more romance injections, idyllic sunsets, and a little more peacefulness, though, we suggest Manchebo Beach on the same side of the island. Action seekers can head towards the eastern shores and beaches like Dos Playa, Boca Prins, Boca Granti, Bachelor’s Beach, Boca Andicuri, and Black Stone Beach that welcome larger waves and stronger currents, laying the table for kitesurfers and windsurfers. As for the coastline on the southeast, it is perfect for families with kids, as well as inexperienced swimmers, due to the calm and shallow waters.
